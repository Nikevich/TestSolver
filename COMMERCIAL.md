## FAQ

**Do I need a commercial license to use TestSolver for my own tests?**
No. Internal use is covered by the AGPL.

**We are a company, and our policy forbids AGPL software. What are our options?**
A commercial license removes the AGPL obligations and is the usual solution. Alternatively, use TestSolver only as a separate tool that is not distributed as part of your product — that does not create AGPL obligations for your own code.

**Can we fork TestSolver and sell it as a closed product?**
Only with a commercial license.

**Can we sell a hosted service built on TestSolver?**
Only with a commercial license, because AGPL-3.0 §13 requires disclosing the source of the service to its users.

**Does the commercial license cover older versions released under MIT?**
Versions published before the dual-licensing change remain available under MIT and are not affected. The commercial license covers versions released under the AGPL/commercial terms.

**Is the license transferable if our company is acquired?**
The license may be transferred together with the product and the part of the business it relates to, with prior written notice.
